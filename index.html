      ABOUT — Ash

      Hello

      I'm an Interaction Designer and Creative Technologist exploring spatial computing, generative interfaces, and tactile web paradigms. I prototype through code, living at the intersection of human cognitive ergonomics, physical feedback loops, and expressive physical software. I create playful instruments, dynamic typography experiments, generative micro-interactions, and tools that challenge conventional interface idioms.

      BACKGROUND

      Previously, I led design engineering initiatives across high-frequency transaction engines, fluid spatial booking flows, and gamified behavioural software. I began my practice in independent visual communication studios, specializing in brand systems, experimental typography, and generative graphic identities.
